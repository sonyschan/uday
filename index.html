The idea

Which days mean something to you?

A birthday. An anniversary. Or a day that changed who you are.

Now they can live onchain. uDAY turns your days — and the days of people you care about — into onchain art assets, each one permanently crafted with its own provenance.

And they are not static. Their appearance can be dynamically regenerated through token trades.

Keep one for yourself. Send one to someone who matters. Each uDAY represents a specific day.

The year

A year, lighting up

Every cell is a day. Lit means at least one uDAY of that date has been born onchain; the brighter, the more of them exist. Tap a day to visit its earliest piece.

Your days

Which days are already yours?

Connect a wallet — or paste your address. The lookup runs entirely in your browser against the same public index the wall uses; nothing is sent anywhere.

The composer

Four layers, two of them optional

Every uDAY is a plate, a frame, a month and a date, painted one on top of the next. Pick a day and watch it assemble — the same order the contract uses.
